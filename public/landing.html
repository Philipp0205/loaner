Lend things to your friends. Borrow what you need.

Loan some is a small app for sharing what you own with people you know. You join private groups with an invite link. Nobody else sees your things.

Get started I have a recovery code

How it works

  1. List it

    Take a photo of something you would lend. The app suggests a name and a category on your phone, so the photo is not sent anywhere for that. You can change both.

  2. Friends ask

    Someone in your group picks the dates and asks to borrow it. You get an alert and say yes or no. Each item has its own messages.

  3. Hand it over

    When it changes hands, tap Handed over. The item shows as lent, with a due date.

  4. Get it back

    When it comes back, tap Returned. It is available again. You both get a reminder the day before it is due.

Friends only

You make a group or join one from a friend's invite link. Being in the group is the trust: anyone in it can borrow what is listed there. You choose which of your groups can see each item, and you can be in several.

There are no passwords. Your phone keeps a device token, and you get a recovery code in case you switch phones.

What it does not do

  • No payments or deposits. For something valuable you can suggest a donation. The app only shows the amount, and money never moves in it.
  • No public listings, and no maps.
  • No ads and no analytics.

Put it on your home screen

Loan some works in your browser, and you can install it like an app. Look for Add to Home Screen or Install in your browser's menu. It follows your phone's light or dark setting.
